Chicken Kasha

Description

This easy recipe makes a fast and tasty meal for busy weeknights.

It uses fresh ingredients and takes only thirty minutes to cook.

Ingredients list

Serves 3 Servings

  • Chicken 500 grams
  • Onion 250 grams
  • Ginger garlic paste 22 grams
  • Spices three types
  • Cilantro 5 to 6 pcs
  • Mustard oil 30 grams
  • Tomato or curd 1 pcs
  • Salt According to taste
  • water
  • Green chili's 6 pcs
  • Chicken Masala

Steps

  1. Fry the onions till it gets brown
  2. Add Ginger garlic paste
  3. Add Spices
  4. Add Chicken
  5. Add Salt
  6. Fry till water vaped
  7. Add tomato or curd
  8. Add some water
  9. Add some Cilantro
  10. Green chili's
  11. Chicken masala
Done
